Inspection Chinese is a static regular-weight subset derived from Noto Sans CJK SC.
Source font: /system/fonts/NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc on the connected Xiaomi 13 Pro.
Upstream: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
License: Assets/Resources/NotoSans-LICENSE.txt (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

Includes Basic Latin, Latin Extended, CJK unified ideographs and Extension A,
Chinese/full-width punctuation. Renamed Inspection Chinese and instantiated at
weight 400 for Unity 2022.3 compatibility. Unused glyphs and variable weights
are omitted to avoid embedding the full 32 MB system collection. Imported as a
dynamic font. Rare supplementary CJK characters outside these ranges are omitted.
